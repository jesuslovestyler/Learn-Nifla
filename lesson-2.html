Step 3: again, while a question holds


Deciding once is half the craft. The other half is repeating. while repeats its stretch as long as its question keeps answering true:





Runs right here, in your browser.




The book says it answers:
3
2
1
Go




Read it as a story: n starts at 3; the question "is n still at least 1?" is asked before every round; inside, the program says n and then makes n smaller — and that making-smaller is what lets the loop ever end. A loop whose question never turns false would run forever, so the language stands guard: a program whose loops pass a million times without finishing is stopped and told so, in plain words.


Your turn. Count up instead of down: start at 1 and say 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, one per line, with a while.
